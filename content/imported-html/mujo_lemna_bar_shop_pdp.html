Common questions. Direct answers.

If yours isn't here, the contact form is two clicks away and a real human will answer.

What does "PDCAAS close to 1.0" actually mean?
PDCAAS is the modern measure of how well your body can use a protein. Whey is around 1.1, soy is around 0.9, most plant proteins sit between 0.5 and 0.8. Lemna leaf comes in close to 1.0, which means almost every gram you eat gets put to use, the same way it would with whey.
Why no sugar alcohols?
Sugar alcohols (erythritol, maltitol, sorbitol, xylitol) are how most "low sugar" protein bars hit their numbers. They also cause bloating and digestive issues for a large portion of people. We use real sweeteners (date syrup, raw honey) at lower amounts. The macros are honest. Your gut is calmer.
Why is one vegan and one not?
PB Crunch uses date syrup. Cashew Chocolate uses raw honey, because honey lifts the cashew and chocolate flavors in a way nothing plant-based did, after 47 batches. So PB Crunch is fully vegan; Cashew is vegetarian. We could have forced both into vegan, but the bar would have been worse, so we didn't.
How is this different from a Larabar or RXBAR?
More protein, and the protein source is different. RXBARs use egg white, which is great if you eat eggs and not great if you don't. Larabars are mostly date and nut, around 6g protein. The Lemna Bar gives you 14–17g of complete-amino-acid plant protein with a similarly short ingredient list. Different goal, different bar.
When does pre-order ship?
First boxes ship in July 2026. We charge your card the day your box leaves Lisbon, not before. You'll get a tracking link the moment it ships.
Allergens?
PB Crunch contains peanuts and oats. Cashew Chocolate contains cashews, oats, and traces of dairy (in the dark chocolate). Both are made in a facility that handles tree nuts. Full allergen statement on every box.
Can I get a sample first?
Pre-order is the sample. The 30-day promise covers your first box: if it's not for you, we refund or swap, no flavor interrogation. Easier than mailing single bars and gives you a real read on it.
Is Lemna leaf safe in pregnancy?
Lemna leaf protein is a food, not a supplement, and there are no known concerns. As always with anything new during pregnancy, run it past your midwife or doctor first.
