From the founder

Get into the head of a founder who researches obsessively, so you don't have to.

Image Placeholder
Kinga · candid
At a padel match, in the kitchen with kids, or at a Rebel Club gathering. Not a headshot.

Wellness has become slightly unhinged. I kept adding steps to my routine, checking my Whoop, and ending up more tired than when I started. The 10-step routine, the optimization stack, the protocol of the week. Somewhere in there, the body got lost.

Then I learned how my nervous system actually works, and realized it's much smarter than me. Most of the time, it knows exactly what to do. The way I was processing inputs kept getting in its way.

The bottom line: you don't need a 10-step routine (unless you're really into that, that's cool too). I want less, but better. The 20% that makes the 80% impact. With three kids, a business, an active lifestyle, and sleep to protect, that's what I have time for.

Twice a month, I send what I'm reading, testing, and rethinking, broken down into insights anyone can understand. Rebel with me. Let's go back to basics and do what actually works, so we have time to live our lives with joy and health. Members shape what Mujo becomes next. Early access, member-only essays, the occasional drop made just for the club.

See you in your inbox.
