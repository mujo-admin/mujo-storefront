The mycelium problem: Most products use mycelium on grain, not actual mushrooms. Beta-(1,3)(1,6)-glucans live in the fruiting body. A product can legally say "mushroom extract" using mycelium with under 5% beta-(1,3)(1,6)-glucan content. Mujo Ritual uses fruiting body at 8:1, standardized for beta-(1,3)(1,6)-glucans. The questions we'd ask if we were you.

Do I need to quit coffee to drink Mujo?
No. Many people just drink less coffee over time. We suggest easing off caffeine over a week or two as you bring Mujo in. The first few mornings off coffee can be an adjustment, and that's completely normal.
What's actually in it?
Everything, on the label, by its plain name and form. No "proprietary blend," nothing hidden. A few real ingredients, openly used, so you always know exactly what's in your cup.
Is it meant to be a daily ritual?
Yes. Adaptogens are gentle and work best as a daily habit, not a one-off jolt. Our honest advice: make it your morning cup and enjoy it. The taste alone is reason enough to keep it.
What does it taste like?
Warm, rich cacao latte, genuinely good, not something you're tolerating. No mushroom taste. No aftertaste. Oat or coconut milk recommended. It's the first functional drink most people say they look forward to.
