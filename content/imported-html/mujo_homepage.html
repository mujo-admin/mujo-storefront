Why it actually works

The ingredients matter. The form matters more.

Most functional brands, and ours, can list the same ingredients. The difference is whether your body can use them. Three things we refused to shortcut:

01 · The mushrooms

Extracted, not just powdered

A mushroom's good stuff is locked inside cell walls your gut can't break down. Eat the raw powder and most of it passes straight through. We extract with both water and oil to pull out the full range, so what's on the label reaches your cells.

Most brands use mycelium grown on grain, because it's cheaper. If the label doesn't say "fruiting body," it probably isn't.

02 · The adaptogens

The dose the studies used

KSM-66® Ashwagandha at 300mg. Rhodiola Rosea standardized to 5% rosavins and 1.5% salidrosides, cultivated in the Himalayas to protect the wild plant. An ingredient only does what the research says when it's the same form and dose.

A lot of products use a fraction of the clinical dose. Enough to print the name. Not enough to feel it.

03 · The curcumin

Built to reach your brain

Ordinary curcumin barely survives digestion and never reaches your brain, so you feel none of the benefit. Ours is specially encapsulated to cross the blood-brain barrier and deliver where it counts. Up to 285x more bioavailable than standard.

Same ingredient, completely different result, because of the form. It's the whole philosophy in one example.

These are the little things almost nobody talks about. They're the whole reason Mujo does something you can feel.

Not every ingredient is clinically studied. The adaptogens and standardized forms are. The mushrooms are chosen for the best form. We'll always tell you which is which.
