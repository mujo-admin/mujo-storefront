Our story

Built in my kitchen. For my own morning.

"I didn't build Mujo to sell a product. I built it because I needed it, and nothing like it existed."

I was a new mom, working 60-hour weeks, and coffee was burning me out. I was exhausted and anxious. Wired at 11pm. Waking up tired and depleted.

Every "mushroom cacao" on the market used mycelium instead of fruiting bodies. Every adaptogen was underdosed or fake. So I started mixing in my kitchen. Real fruiting body extracts. Organic Peruvian cacao. Clinically studied adaptogens at their research-validated doses.

Mujo came from a refusal to accept that high performance should leave you depleted. I built it as a ritual for my nervous system: neurowellness. Your morning should restore you, not run you down.
