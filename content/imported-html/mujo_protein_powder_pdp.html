Meet Lemna leaf

A tiny plant, grown on water.

Yes. It has all nine essential amino acids, and every one is above the FAO/WHO reference. Each 22g serving has 9.8g of essential amino acids, 4.3g of BCAAs and 1,940mg of leucine.
How long does a pouch last?
Each pouch has 15 servings, so about two weeks if you have one a day. It works out at $3.00 a serving, about 13.6¢ per gram of protein.
What are the allergens?
It's vegan and free from gluten, dairy and soy. The MCT oil powder is made from coconut, and it's made in a facility that also processes peanuts and tree nuts. If you have an allergy, email us before you order and we'll help.
Can I bake with it?
Yes. Stir it into oats, pancake batter or anything you'd add a powder to. For a drink, it's made for cold liquid in a blender.
How do I store it?
Keep the pouch sealed in a cool, dry cupboard. No fridge needed. The best-before date is printed on the back.
