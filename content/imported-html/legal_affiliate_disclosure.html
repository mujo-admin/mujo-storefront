Ambassador Program Disclosure

Per U.S. Federal Trade Commission regulations (16 CFR Part 255), we disclose our material connections with ambassadors and affiliates who promote Mujo products. This page sets out the commercial relationship, the disclosure obligations we place on our ambassadors, and how to report concerns.

1. What the Mujo Ambassador Program is

The Mujo Ambassador Program is a selective partnership program. We invite individuals and organizations — including studios, padel and athletic clubs, creators, practitioners, and community leaders — to promote Mujo products in exchange for compensation. Ambassadors operate as independent third parties, not as Mujo employees or representatives.

Program eligibility is by application and review. Details and the application form are at mujoworld.com/ambassador.

2. Compensation

Ambassadors earn a flat 15% commission on qualifying sales made through their unique discount code or tracked referral link.

Commission structure:

  • Rate: 15% flat, paid on the subtotal of qualifying orders (excluding taxes and shipping).
  • Recurring on subscriptions: commission is paid on subscription renewals from referred customers for the lifetime of the subscription. This follows standard premium-DTC practice and reflects the ongoing value ambassadors deliver when their referrals stay on subscription.
  • Payout cadence: monthly, in arrears, for commissions earned in the previous calendar month.
  • Payout methods: bank transfer, PayPal, or Stripe — ambassador's choice.
  • Qualifying sales: sales attributed to the ambassador's unique code or link, where the order completes and is not refunded. Refunded orders and chargebacks are clawed back from the commission balance.

In addition to commission, ambassadors may receive: free product for personal use, early access to new launches and product drops, invitations to ambassador-only events, and ambassador-exclusive Rebel Club benefits.

3. FTC disclosure requirement for ambassadors

Per FTC §16 CFR 255.5 (Disclosure of Material Connections), ambassadors must clearly disclose their material connection to Mujo in every post, reel, story, video, email, blog, podcast mention, or written piece that promotes or mentions Mujo products.

Acceptable disclosures include:

  • #MujoAmbassador
  • #ad or #sponsored (use in addition to, not instead of, ambassador-specific disclosure)
  • "Paid partnership with Mujo"
  • "I'm a Mujo ambassador and receive a commission if you purchase through my link"
  • Equivalent clear and conspicuous language

Disclosures must be:

  • Clear — in plain language a reasonable consumer would understand
  • Conspicuous — visible without the reader having to click "more," scroll past, tap to expand, or hunt through hashtags
  • Close to the claim — in the same post, caption, video, or written piece as the promotional content, not buried in a linked bio or separate page
  • Platform-appropriate — in video, included verbally and on-screen; in audio, stated in the episode; in static posts, within the visible caption before the "more" cut

4. What ambassadors cannot do

Ambassadors agree not to:

  • Make unauthorized health claims about Mujo products. Mujo products are dietary supplements and functional foods, not medical treatment, and may not be represented as diagnosing, treating, curing, or preventing any disease.
  • Make claims Mujo itself does not make, including clinical, therapeutic, or outcome-specific claims beyond the approved talking points.
  • Misrepresent the product, its ingredients, dosages, or effects.
  • Imply personal endorsement if compensated, without making the required disclosure.
  • Represent themselves as Mujo employees, agents, or spokespeople.
  • Use Mujo's trademarks, brand assets, or imagery outside the guidelines provided in the ambassador toolkit.
  • Engage in coupon-code abuse, fake traffic generation, or any form of commission fraud.

Violations may result in termination from the program, clawback of commissions paid on non-compliant activity, and reporting to the relevant platform or regulator where applicable.

5. Our responsibilities

Mujo commits to:

  • Provide ambassadors with accurate product information, a claim-safe talking-points document, approved imagery, and an onboarding FTC-compliance briefing.
  • Train ambassadors on claim discipline (FDA-safe language), disclosure requirements (FTC §16 CFR 255.5), and brand guidelines.
  • Monitor ambassador content for compliance, using both manual review and third-party monitoring tools where appropriate.
  • Communicate promptly when content is non-compliant and give ambassadors a reasonable opportunity to correct the issue.
  • Terminate partnerships that repeatedly violate policy or that violate policy in ways that cannot be corrected.
  • Pay commissions on time and on the terms agreed at the time of the sale.

6. How to report concerns

If you notice an undisclosed ambassador post, an ambassador making claims outside Mujo's approved guidelines, or any other concern related to the program, please email kinga@mujo.life with:

  • The ambassador name or handle
  • A link or screenshot of the content
  • A short description of the concern

We review every report, usually within 5 business days, and take action as needed.

7. Changes

Material changes to the ambassador program — including changes to commission rates, disclosure requirements, or eligibility — are notified to active ambassadors at least 30 days in advance by email. This public disclosure page is updated at the same time, and the "Last updated" date at the top reflects the current version.
