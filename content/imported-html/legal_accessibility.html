Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making Mujo usable by everyone, and how to tell us when we fall short.

1. Our commitment

Mujo Co. is committed to making mujoworld.com accessible to all visitors, including people who use assistive technology. We target conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. Everyone should be able to browse, learn about, and order our products regardless of ability.

2. What we’ve done

Specific steps taken to meet our WCAG 2.1 AA target:

  • Color contrast ratios meet WCAG thresholds: at least 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text.
  • The site is fully keyboard-navigable: tab, enter, and escape keys work across all interactive elements.
  • Semantic HTML throughout: correct heading hierarchy, landmarks (header, nav, main, footer), and lists.
  • Alt text on all product images and meaningful illustrations. Decorative images are marked with empty alt attributes.
  • Form labels are paired with inputs. Error messages are linked via aria-describedby so screen readers announce them.
  • Visible focus indicators on every interactive element.
  • Tested with common screen readers: NVDA (Windows), VoiceOver (macOS and iOS), and TalkBack (Android).

3. Known issues and remediation

We’re not perfect yet. Current known gaps:

  • Some third-party embeds, notably Okendo review widgets and some video players, may have partial accessibility depending on how their vendors ship updates. We monitor and flag issues to those vendors.
  • Legacy blog posts published before April 2026 may lack complete alt-text or heading structure. These are being remediated during our Journal voice-polish pass.
  • PDFs distributed from the site are not guaranteed accessible. Where possible, we provide an HTML version alongside any PDF.

4. How to report an issue

If you run into an accessibility barrier on our site, please tell us. Email kinga@mujo.life with:

  • The URL of the page where you encountered the issue.
  • A description of the problem.
  • Any assistive technology you were using (screen reader, browser, operating system), if relevant.

We respond within 5 business days. For issues that block you from placing an order or completing a critical task, we aim to ship a fix within 30 days.

5. Ongoing work

Accessibility is a moving target, not a one-time checkbox. Our ongoing commitments:

  • Quarterly internal accessibility audits covering all primary purchase flows.
  • Annual third-party accessibility audits planned after our W3 platform cutover.
  • Training content creators on alt-text, heading structure, and plain-language copy standards.

6. Contact

General accessibility questions or feedback: kinga@mujo.life. For urgent issues affecting order placement, DM us on Instagram @mujoworld for a faster response.
