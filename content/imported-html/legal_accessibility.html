Accessibility. Built to be usable by everyone.

Our commitment to an accessible site, the standard we work toward, and how to tell us when something isn’t working for you so we can fix it.

Mujo Co. (“Mujo”, “we”, “us”) wants mujoworld.com to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, voice control, or screen magnification.

1. Our commitment

Accessibility is part of doing this properly, not an afterthought. We treat it as ongoing work: as we add and update pages, we try to make them perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust for everyone, and we fix issues when we find them or when you report them.

2. The standard we work toward

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, the standard widely referenced for accessible websites. “Aim to conform” is honest: we work toward this standard and measure ourselves against it, but we don’t claim every page meets every criterion at all times, particularly where third-party tools are involved.

3. What we do

Steps we take as part of building and maintaining the site include:

  • using semantic, structured HTML with proper headings and landmarks;
  • providing text alternatives for meaningful images;
  • supporting keyboard navigation and visible focus;
  • aiming for sufficient color contrast in our palette and type;
  • writing in plain language wherever the subject allows;
  • and designing layouts that adapt to mobile screens and larger text.

4. Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some content may not yet be fully accessible. Known and potential gaps include features provided by third parties (such as embedded reviews, checkout, or media players) whose accessibility we don’t fully control, and older or complex content we haven’t yet revisited. We’re working to address these, and we’ll keep this section updated as our review progresses.

5. Tell us what’s not working

If you run into a barrier on our site, or you need information in a different format, please tell us — it genuinely helps us improve, and we’ll do our best to provide what you need. Contact us at hello@mujoworld.com, and please include the page, what you were trying to do, and the assistive technology or browser you’re using so we can reproduce and fix it. We aim to respond within a few business days.

6. Ongoing effort

We review accessibility as the site evolves and update this statement as we make improvements or identify new issues. You can also reach us by mail at Mujo Co., 433 W. Harrison St #5409, SMB #30446, Chicago IL 60699.

Questions about this? Email hello@mujoworld.com or message us through the contact page. Real human, working day reply.
