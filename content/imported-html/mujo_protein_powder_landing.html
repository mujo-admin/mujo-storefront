Cacao Peanut Butter Shake
our everyday favorite
  • 2 scoops MUJO Protein Powder, Vanilla
  • 10 oz (300ml) milk of your choice
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp cacao powder
  • 2 tbsp Greek yogurt or cream cheese

Method. Blend for about a minute, until thick and smooth.
