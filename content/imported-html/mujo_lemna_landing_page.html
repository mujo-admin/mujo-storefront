The hero ingredient

Meet Lemna leaf.

Lemna leaf is the smallest flowering plant in the world. It has been studied for decades but only recently became commercially viable for human nutrition.

Lemna leaf has a complete amino acid profile, comparable to whey on every metric that matters for muscle protein synthesis. It even has a perfect digestibility score of 1.0, like eggs and whey. But unlike whey, soy, or pea, Lemna grows on water in closed tanks, requires no arable land, and doubles its biomass every 24 to 48 hours.

Why this bar exists

It started with my sisters.

I have two sisters. One has celiac. One has multiple food allergies. Their snack drawer is limited, but their high-performer lives demand high-quality, conscious nutrition on the go. Most bars left them bloated, made them sick, or offended their taste. And because I love making healthy snacks in my kitchen, I decided to surprise them with a protein bar.

They hated the first flavor. They told me to stop. I refused. About fifty attempts later, I handed them a bar. My sister Gabi closed her eyes, smiled, and said: "cake." The other tasters looked surprised, in a good way. A few actually moaned.

Lemna is basically a swamp plant that surprised us all by being such an incredible protein.

Making a clean bar wasn't easy. Flavor-first, macro-amazing, and good for your gut is a complicated dance. Then came the co-manufacturer delays. The endless "you can't make a bar this way." "You need allulose." "You need natural flavors." The sleepless nights when I felt like giving up. But my sisters needed this bar. So I kept going.

Quick questions, quick answers.

The things people ask most.

What is Lemna leaf, actually?
Lemna minor is the smallest flowering plant in the world. It's a complete protein source with a PDCAAS of 1.0, equivalent to egg and whey, comparable to whey for muscle protein synthesis. We source ours from Plantible Foods (Rubi Protein™), grown in closed-loop water systems with no arable land and no pesticides. It's a leaf with a complete amino acid profile. The naming matters because the plant matters.
When does the bar ship?
Summer/fall 2026. Founding members get the launch link 24 hours before the public, with 15% off your first order locked in. We'll email you the moment the launch goes live.
Is it gluten-free?
Yes. The bar is gluten-free. It's produced in a facility that handles other allergens, so we'll publish the full allergen statement at launch.
Is it vegan?
Yes. The bar is fully vegan. Full nutritional and allergen breakdown will be on the product page at launch.
Why is there vegetable glycerin in a "clean" bar?
Honest answer: vegetable glycerin (from coconut) is what keeps a real-ingredient bar soft on the shelf without refrigeration or preservatives. It's the cleanest workable solution we found. We could have left it off the marketing copy. We'd rather you knew before you bought.
