The 8:1 concentration, explained
+
Our mushrooms are 8:1 dual-extracted, meaning 8 parts raw mushroom are concentrated into 1 part of active bioactive compounds. 1,200mg of 8:1 extract delivers the bioactive equivalent of 9,600mg of raw mushroom. This ratio ensures each scoop delivers a clinically meaningful dose of beta-glucans, triterpenoids, and hericenones, in their most bioavailable form.

Blend 1–2 tsp with 200ml hot water or warm milk. Froth, stir, or shake. Sip slowly. It's designed to feel as good as it works.
Quality + sourcing
+
Third-party tested for purity, heavy metals, and potency. Our mushrooms are extracted from the fruiting body only, never mycelium grown on grain. Here's why extraction matters: a mushroom's active compounds sit locked inside cell walls made of chitin, and the human gut can't break chitin down. Eat raw or powdered Lion's Mane or Chaga and most of it passes straight through. Extraction opens those cell walls and pulls the compounds out so your body can absorb them. Some are water-soluble (like beta-glucans) and some are oil-soluble (like triterpenes), so we use both hot-water and alcohol extraction to capture the full range.

Why it actually works

The ingredients matter. The form matters more.

Most functional brands, and ours, can list the same ingredients. The difference is whether your body can use them. Three things we refused to shortcut:

01 · The mushrooms

Extracted, not just powdered

A mushroom's good stuff is locked inside cell walls your gut can't break down. Eat the raw powder and most of it passes straight through. We extract with both water and oil to pull out the full range, so what's on the label reaches your cells.

Most brands use mycelium grown on grain, because it's cheaper. If the label doesn't say "fruiting body," it probably isn't.

02 · The adaptogens

The dose the studies used

KSM-66® Ashwagandha at 300mg. Rhodiola Rosea standardized to 5% rosavins and 1.5% salidrosides, cultivated in the Himalayas to protect the wild plant. An ingredient only does what the research says when it's the same form and dose.

A lot of products use a fraction of the clinical dose. Enough to print the name. Not enough to feel it.

03 · The curcumin

Built to reach your brain

Ordinary curcumin barely survives digestion and never reaches your brain, so you feel none of the benefit. Ours is specially encapsulated to cross the blood-brain barrier and deliver where it counts. Up to 285x more bioavailable than standard.

Same ingredient, completely different result, because of the form. It's the whole philosophy in one example.

These are the little things almost nobody talks about. They're the whole reason Mujo does something you can feel.

What you're wondering.

Am I actually going to feel anything? Or is this just expensive cocoa?
+
Fair question, we've read the same reviews of other brands. Here's the honest answer: you're not going to feel a buzz. That's the point. Caffeine gives you a feeling-on-demand followed by the inevitable crash. Adaptogens work differently. What you'll notice, over weeks, not minutes, is the 3pm wall getting quieter. The anxious hum softening. Sleep getting deeper. Most customers notice a real shift in week two. Some take four. If 30 days in you don't feel anything, full refund. No forms.
How quickly will I notice?
+
Adaptogens build effect over weeks, not minutes. Sleep quality tends to shift within 7–10 days. Reduced afternoon crash in weeks 2–3. Baseline anxiety and cognitive clarity in weeks 3–6. Timeline compounds, the systems these ingredients work on are connected, so the effect tends to accelerate after the first month.
Does it taste like coffee?
+
No. Mujo doesn't try to replicate coffee. It tastes like a warm, rich cacao latte, deep chocolate, smooth, a little earthy in a good way. No mushroom taste. No bitter aftertaste. Oat or coconut milk recommended. Natural sweetness from cacao and cinnamon, no added sugar. The most common feedback is "I didn't expect it to taste this good." If you're looking for something that tastes like coffee, there are better options. If you're looking for a morning ritual that does something different, this is it.
Will I lose anything by quitting caffeine?
+
Probably for the first week, yes. That's your adenosine receptors coming back online. Most people find the dependency headache and low-energy mornings fade in 7–10 days. After that, the energy you had on caffeine is replaced by a steadiness you didn't know was possible. We recommend reducing caffeine gradually while introducing Mujo, not cold turkey. Many customers run Mujo + half their usual coffee for the first month, then naturally drop the coffee.
