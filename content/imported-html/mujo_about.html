A short founder note

Mujo started with my dad's diagnosis. Then a question.

👩
Kinga · Founder · Portugal

When my dad was diagnosed with cancer, my mum and my sister threw themselves into herbalism. They learned what plants and fungi could actually do. By the time I had my third child in 2021 and coffee was the only thing holding my mornings together, I'd grown up around their work, and I'd quietly absorbed a lot of it.

So I made myself a cup of cacao with the things I knew worked. The first one was awful. The fortieth was the one I drink now, and the one thousands of other people drink most mornings.

I wasn't trying to start a brand. I was trying to make something I'd actually drink every morning. Something that worked, and tasted like a proper ritual, not medicine.
The Lemna chapter

Then my sisters asked the same question about protein.

I have two younger sisters. One has celiac disease. The other has a list of food intolerances long enough to put on a card. Between them, almost every protein bar on the shelf was off the table. Too much soy, too many fillers, gut irritants disguised as gut support.

That's how the Lemna Bar started. We needed a clean, complete protein source that didn't trade off taste for label virtue. We found Lemna leaf, a plant protein with a complete amino acid profile, a DIAAS close to 1.0, and roughly ten times less land use than soy or whey. It's the work of a small team in California who've been growing it indoors for a decade.

Then we went into development. 47 batches before we had a bar I'd put my name on. Two flavours, dialled until both of them tasted like something you'd actually want at 4pm. Pre-orders open this summer.

Standards we hold

The things we won't compromise on.

Stubbornness, but the productive kind. These are the lines we drew on day one and haven't moved since.

  • 01
    Clinical doses, not theatre.
    If a study used 300mg, we use 300mg. No "proprietary blends" hiding sub-therapeutic amounts behind marketing language.
  • 02
    Real fruiting bodies. Always.
    Mycelium-on-grain is a different ingredient with a different chemistry. We use the part the studies tested.
  • 03
    Patented strains where they matter.
    KSM-66, RhodioLife, Longvida®. The standardised, third-party-verified versions. Cheaper alternatives exist. We don't use them.
  • 04
    No fillers. No fakery.
    No maltodextrin to bulk out servings. No "natural flavours" doing the heavy lifting. No hidden sugar dressed as something else.
  • 05
    Tested every batch.
    Heavy metals, microbial counts, active compound verification. Independent lab. Results on file.
  • 06
    Cited claims. Always.
    If we say it does something, we'll show you the paper. The good ones, with proper sample sizes, not the cherry-picked ones.
