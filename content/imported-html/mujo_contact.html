How long does shipping take?
US orders ship in 3–5 working days. Free shipping on US orders over $100. We send a tracking link the moment it leaves our US warehouse.
Can I skip or pause my subscription?
Yes, anytime, in two taps from your account. Skip a delivery, swap to a different SKU, change frequency, or pause. We don't bury it three menus deep.

It launches summer 2026. Pre-orders open in July. Sign up to the Sunday Note and you'll be the first to hear, with first-day pricing.
Is Mujo Ritual safe in pregnancy?
Some of the adaptogens (notably ashwagandha and rhodiola) aren't recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. We say so on the label and we mean it. Check with your healthcare provider before starting.
What if I don't love it?
First-time orders are covered by our 30-day promise. If it's not for you, message us and we'll refund or swap. No flavour interrogation.

We currently only ship to the US. Sign up to the Rebel Club to know when we launch other regions in the world.
