Subscription Terms

These Subscription Terms apply to Mujo Ritual and any other Mujo product sold on a recurring basis. They form part of our Terms of Service.

1. How Mujo subscriptions work

When you sign up for a Mujo subscription, you authorize us to automatically bill your chosen payment method and ship a new order on a recurring cycle.

  • Billing cycle: every 30 days, by default. (If we add additional cadences in the future, you can choose one when you subscribe.)
  • Billing amount: the current subscription price at the time of each renewal.
  • Payment processor: all subscriptions are processed via Stripe Billing. Mujo does not store credit or debit card details.

2. Pricing

Price lock. The subscription price you sign up at is locked for your active subscription. If we change subscription pricing, we notify you at least 30 days before the change takes effect, and you can cancel before the change if you prefer.

Taxes (VAT, sales tax) are calculated at the time of each renewal and may vary based on your shipping address. Current pricing is shown on the Mujo Ritual product page and the shop.

3. Shipping

Subscription orders ship using the same timing and carrier options as non-subscription orders. See the Shipping Policy for delivery windows, carriers, and regional specifics. Subscription orders contribute to the free-shipping threshold the same way one-time orders do.

4. Managing your subscription

You manage your subscription through the Stripe Billing Portal. To access the portal, request an email magic link from your account page. We send it to the email on file, and clicking the link opens your portal for a short secure session.

From the portal you can:

  • Skip your next shipment
  • Pause your subscription (resume any time)
  • Change your shipping address
  • Update your payment method
  • Change frequency (pre-launch feature flag, may be limited to the 30-day default at launch)
  • Cancel your subscription

Changes apply to your next renewal if you make them before the renewal date. Changes made after renewal apply to the cycle after that.

5. Payment failures

If your payment method fails to charge on renewal, Stripe retries the payment automatically.

  • Retry schedule: Stripe retries the payment 3 times over approximately 7 days per the Stripe Billing default.
  • Notifications: you receive an email from Stripe on each retry attempt so you can update your payment method in time.
  • After 3 failed retries: your subscription is paused and we email you to update your payment method.
  • After 14 days with no update: the subscription is cancelled automatically. You can resubscribe at any time.

6. Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time via the Stripe Billing Portal. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period, so you receive the product you have already been billed for. There are no cancellation fees.

If you cancel mid-cycle, your subscription remains active until the end of the cycle, then ends automatically. No further charges apply.

7. Refunds on subscription products

  • First order: covered by our 30-day satisfaction guarantee per the Returns Policy.
  • Subsequent renewals: final. However, we review cancellation-adjacent refund requests on a case-by-case basis: for example, if a renewal charged after you believed you had cancelled, or if there is a shipping or product issue.

To request a review, email hello@mujoworld.com with your order number and a short description.

8. Changes to subscription terms

We may update these Subscription Terms from time to time. Material changes, including price changes, billing-cycle changes, or changes that affect your rights as a subscriber, are notified at least 30 days in advance by email. Continued subscription after the notice period constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Subscription questions, billing issues, portal access problems? Email hello@mujoworld.com.
