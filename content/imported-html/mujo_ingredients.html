Ingredient deep dive
On Lion's Mane: what the research actually says

Most mushroom coffee alternatives on the market use around 500 mg of lion's mane powder per serving. That's well below any dose that has shown cognitive effects in human trials. The clinical evidence sits much higher: from 1,800 mg/day to 3,200 mg/day (Mori 2009, Saitsu 2019, Docherty 2023). All of it fruiting body. None of it mycelium-on-grain.

We use 500 mg of an 8:1 fruiting body extract. That's equivalent to 4,000 mg of raw mushroom, standardised for β-glucans, no grain fillers, no mycelium padding. It's the form and the concentration the research actually uses, not the powder-by-weight number that fits neatly on a label.

The principles

What guides our ingredient choices.

Three rules govern every ingredient decision at Mujo. They're not marketing copy, they're the reason the formula looks the way it does.

01 / Fruiting body only
The correct part of the mushroom.
A mushroom has two parts: the mycelium, the underground root network, and the fruiting body, the cap that pushes above ground. The bioactive compounds people have eaten mushrooms for thousands of years to access, the beta-glucans, the triterpenes, the cordycepin, sit in the fruiting body. Most mushroom coffees use mycelium grown on sterilized oats or sorghum, then mill the whole substrate, oats included. The result is mostly starch with trace actives. Mujo uses fruiting body extracts only, dual-extracted at 8:1 concentration, with beta-glucans verified each batch.
02 / Clinically backed, actually
Not kitchen-quality curcuma.
When we built this blend, we reached for natural ingredients enhanced by science so we can maximize bioavailability, make sure your body can use the active compounds, and that the active compounds are actually present in the dose where the research found an effect. The patented curcumin form, the 300mg ashwagandha dose, the premium-standardised rhodiola, every choice is keyed to what the clinical literature actually tested. Generic versions of the same plant rarely produce the same outcomes. To be straight with you: not every ingredient here has its own clinical trial. The adaptogens and standardized forms do. The mushrooms we choose for their form. We'll always tell you which is which.
03 / Sustainability matters
Respect the source.
We're tapping into Mother Nature to deliver these benefits, so we want to do our part to respect her. We work with ingredient suppliers who source mindfully, harvest at the optimal time, and cultivate where they can rather than draw from wild supply. Wild-harvested chaga from sustainably managed forests. Organic Peruvian cacao. Himalayan rhodiola, cultivated to protect the species. Traceable origin for every ingredient on the label.
