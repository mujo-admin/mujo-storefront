Pre-order questions.

The things people ask most.

When do I get charged?
Your card is not charged at pre-order. We hold the order, lock in your founding member pricing, and only charge you when the bar ships in summer 2026. You can cancel any time before ship.
Why is it called a pre-order if I'm not paying yet?
Because you're reserving your founding member rate. The 15% founding discount is locked the moment you reserve, for the life of the SKU. Once the launch goes public, that pricing is gone. Pre-order is how we honor early supporters without making you commit financially before we deliver.
Can I change my flavor or pack size before it ships?
Yes, any time before the ship date. We'll send you a confirmation email about a month before launch with a link to update your selection.
Is the bar gluten-free?
Yes, both flavors are gluten-free. Full allergen statement will be on the package and the PDP at launch.
Is it vegan?
Peanut Butter Crunch is fully vegan (uses date syrup as the binder). Cashew Chocolate is vegetarian but not vegan (uses raw honey).
What's in vegetable glycerin and why is it on the label?
It's from coconut. Vegetable glycerin keeps the bar soft on the shelf without preservatives or refrigeration. It's the cleanest workable solution we found for a real-ingredient bar with a reasonable shelf life. We name it on the label and on this page because we'd rather you knew.
Will there be a subscription option?
Yes, available at launch. Subscription gets a small additional discount on top of your founding member rate. Cancel any time, change cadence any time.
Where do you ship?
United States at launch. EU rollout follows in late 2026 (Portugal, Netherlands first). If you're outside the US and want updates on EU launch, sign up and we'll route you to the right list.
What if I want to learn more about Lemna leaf?
Read the full ingredient breakdown on the Ingredients page or the deep science on the Science page. Lemna minor is the smallest flowering plant in the world and a complete protein source with a DIAAS score near 1.0.
